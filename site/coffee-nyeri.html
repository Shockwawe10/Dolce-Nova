Origin · Kenya

Nyeri, on the slopes of Mount Kenya

Nyeri lies on the south-western slopes of Mount Kenya, where red volcanic soil, high ground and two rainy seasons a year give Kenyan coffee its bright, juicy acidity.

Smallholders deliver their cherries to washing stations, called factories, run by their cooperative; Gatomboya is one of them. Careful sorting, washing, soaking and slow drying on raised tables are why Kenyan coffees taste so clean.
