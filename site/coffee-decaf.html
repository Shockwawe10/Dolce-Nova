Origin · Colombia

Colombia’s coffee mountains

Our decaf is Colombian coffee from the mountains of the central Andes. Colombia’s coffee belt runs along the cordilleras, and the hills of Quindío, pictured, are part of its Coffee Cultural Landscape, a UNESCO World Heritage site.

The green coffee is decaffeinated in Colombia with ethyl acetate made from fermented sugarcane: the beans are steamed, washed in it until the caffeine has gone, then steamed and dried again. It is gentle, and the coffee keeps its sweetness.
